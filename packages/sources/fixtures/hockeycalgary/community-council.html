Community Council

Home > PROGRAMS > Community

The Community stream is Hockey Calgary's largest stream of hockey.

Community Council oversees all teams and leagues in the U7 (Timbits), U9, U11 and U13 age categories. They also oversee tiered leagues in U15 and U18 lower than AA and a U21-C league.
